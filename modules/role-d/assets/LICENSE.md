# Original demo assets — CC0

All seven WAV cues and all five SVG illustrations are original procedural assets generated by `scripts/generate-assets.py` for this repository. No external recordings, music, stock imagery, or real family photographs were used. To the extent possible under law, these assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No attribution or royalty is required.

Sounds: 22,050 Hz, 16-bit mono PCM WAV, quiet sine tones with smooth envelopes, under one second each. Names match the seven contract states: idle, listening, thinking, speaking, happy, worried, alert. A should respect browser audio-unlock requirements and volume preferences.

The five SVGs are clearly labeled family-photo placeholders, not actual photographs. Replace them with family-approved photos through Settings in B/C's integration. No personal photos were accessed.
